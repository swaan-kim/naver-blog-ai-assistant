# Performance review guide

Read only for D+7, D+28, or the once-a-month manual review.

For each post, record a D+7 and D+28 snapshot in `performance-log.csv`: views, search-inflow share, actual inflow terms, comments/likes, and writing/review minutes. Record monthly blog-level revisit rate, average visit count, and net new neighbors only in the monthly review; do not pretend they are post metrics.

At D+1, confirm public visibility, title, hub link, and semantic next link. At D+90, review time-sensitive plan, price, availability, policy, and security claims and either update the visible checked date or mark the article stale.

Do not rename Naver's post-view or inflow data as CTR, completion rate, or conversion. The first month establishes a baseline; do not invent a view target.

Compare only posts of the same format or paired role and record exactly:

- one thing to keep;
- one thing to change;
- one experiment for next month.

Compare search, scene, and reward title patterns; primary-audience and design/PM topics; entry and proof posts; and expected versus actual search terms. Change one editorial variable at a time. This is an operational observation, not an A/B test or causal proof.

Use `assets/monthly-review-template.md` and `assets/performance-log-template.csv`. Never claim causation from a single month's descriptive data.
