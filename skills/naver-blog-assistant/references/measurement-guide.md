# Monthly review guide

Read only for the once-a-month manual review.

Ask the owner for these Naver values: post views, inflow paths, inflow search terms, monthly revisit rate, average visit count, comments/likes, net new neighbors, and perceived writing/review burden.

Do not rename Naver's post-view or inflow data as CTR, completion rate, or conversion. The first month establishes a baseline; do not invent a view target.

Compare only posts of the same format and record exactly:

- one thing to keep;
- one thing to change;
- one experiment for next month.

Use `assets/monthly-review-template.md`. Never claim causation from a single month's descriptive data.
