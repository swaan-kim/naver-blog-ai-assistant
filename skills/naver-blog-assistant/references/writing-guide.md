# AI 냠냠 writing guide

## Public voice and evidence

- The owner is the only public narrator. 솜솜 researches, checks, and edits silently.
- Promise: explain AI plainly; recommend only after direct use.
- Lead with a workplace question or scene. Avoid character dialogue, excess emoji, and repeated summaries.
- Separate sourced fact, owner experience, interpretation, and unknowns. Never invent experience, outcomes, or certainty.
- Prefer official, paper, or public-agency evidence. Use independent reporting for context; never use rumors alone.
- Put the one-line answer in the first two sentences. A reader should understand the benefit without reading the background.

## Choose one format

### 3분 AI 정리

900–1,400 characters, 3–4 headings: change → relevance → plain explanation → unknowns → question.

### 직접 써봤습니다

1,500–2,100 characters, 4–5 headings: goal → help → friction → reuse → fit. State usage level and use the four owner notes.

### 자료를 확인해봤습니다

1,500–2,100 characters, 4–5 headings: question → facts → meaning → limits → impact. High-stakes topics get explanation, not advice.

## Shape and layout

- Title: reader question plus benefit; no series prefix and no duplicate title in the body.
- First screen, in this order: `이런 분께`, `읽는 시간`, `한 줄 답`, `이 글에서 볼 것`.
- Generate the internal table of contents from the approved H2 headings; keep the order identical.
- One idea and 1–3 sentences per paragraph. Leave a blank line before and after every heading.
- Indent lists only one level; use at most one table. Do not restate a table in prose.
- Naver: Nanum Gothic 15 pt, left aligned, 180% line spacing.
- Use one cover and one real screen or diagram. Use the supplied mascot or a text-only cover.
- Set `ai_media: "true"` for AI-generated or edited media.
- End with one useful next action and one concrete reader question, not a generic summary.
- Show sources as `확인한 자료(기준일)` or inline links.
- Give one reusable checklist, decision rule, comparison, or test. Do not pad.

Patch only the requested section after approval. Do not regenerate accepted sections.

Every post needs one visible proof: owner screenshot, before/after, failure and correction, small test, workflow result, table, or original diagram. Record source, capture date, use basis, and AI-edit status.
