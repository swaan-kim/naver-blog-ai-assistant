# AI 냠냠 writing guide

## Public voice and evidence

- The owner narrates; 솜솜 researches and edits silently. Explain AI plainly and recommend only after direct use.
- Lead with a workplace scene and the one-line answer. Avoid character dialogue, excess emoji, and repeated summaries.
- Separate sourced fact, owner experience, interpretation, and unknowns. Never invent experience or outcomes.
- Prefer official, paper, or public-agency evidence; use independent reporting only for context.

## Choose one format

### 3분 AI 정리

900–1,400 characters, 3–4 headings: change → relevance → explanation → unknowns.

### 직접 써봤습니다

1,500–2,100 characters, 4–5 headings: goal → help → friction → reuse → fit. Include usage level and four owner notes.

### 자료를 확인해봤습니다

1,500–2,100 characters, 4–5 headings: question → facts → meaning → limits → impact. High-stakes topics get explanation, not advice.

## Shape and layout

- Title: reader question plus benefit; no series prefix and no duplicate title in the body.
- First screen, in this order: `이런 분께`, `읽는 시간`, `한 줄 답`, `이 글에서 볼 것`.
- Generate the table of contents from approved H2s in the same order.
- One idea and 1–3 sentences per paragraph. Leave a blank line before and after every heading.
- Use one-level lists and at most one table; do not repeat the table in prose.
- Naver: Nanum Gothic 15 pt, left aligned, 180% line spacing.
- Default to one quiet cover. Add one screen or diagram only when prose or a small table is insufficient.
- Never place images consecutively. Keep the mascot small, use whitespace and short labels.
- Set `ai_media: "true"` for AI-generated or edited media.
- End with one action and one concrete question, not a generic summary.
- Show sources as `확인한 자료(기준일)` or inline links.
- Give one reusable checklist, decision rule, comparison, or test. Do not pad.

Patch only the requested section after approval. Do not regenerate accepted sections.

Every post needs proof, not necessarily an image: screenshot, before/after, failure and correction, small test, workflow, table, or original diagram. Record its source, date, use basis, and AI-edit status. If text or a table is enough, leave `evidence_image` empty and omit `{{image:evidence}}`.
