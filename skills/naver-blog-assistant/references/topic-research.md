# Topic research

Read only when the owner asks for topics, trends, search questions, or `이번 주 AI 냠냠 시작`.

## Audience and promise

Primary reader: a nondeveloper in planning, marketing, operations, or CX who has tried AI but not connected it to a workflow. Secondary readers are designers and junior PMs.

Promise: explain AI plainly and recommend only after direct verification.

## Candidate search

Check the shared index before searching. Use:

- official announcements, papers, and public-agency material from the previous 14 days;
- Naver autocomplete or DataLab as relative demand signals, never absolute search volume;
- an evergreen backlog of repeated workplace questions;
- normalized titles and reader questions already in the index.

Never use rumor or a social post alone. High-stakes topics require authoritative and reliable independent sources; explain without personal advice.

Return five candidates only. For each show:

1. the concrete workplace scene;
2. why it matters now;
3. source level and date;
4. one unique proof the owner can show;
5. how it differs from an existing post;
6. the paired practical follow-up;
7. what must be rechecked before publication.

Reject a candidate when its core claim lacks a primary source, it needs invented experience, it duplicates an existing question, or no visible proof or practical follow-up is possible.

## Four pilot clusters

Use one `entry` post and one `proof` post per cluster:

1. repetitive work: meeting, research, report;
2. self-service: browser automation, landing page, prototype;
3. safety: company files, permissions, API keys;
4. result quality: AI voice, design sameness, guardrails.

Across the first eight posts, six target planning/marketing/operations/CX and two target design/PM. Space posts two to four days apart; never batch-publish.

## Title set

Make exactly three materially different titles:

- search: the question a reader would type;
- scene: a recognizable failure or mismatch;
- reward: the decision or action gained after reading.

Use one concept and one concrete payoff. Tension must come from a verified gap between expectation and reality. Avoid `충격`, `무조건`, `모르면 손해`, unsupported numbers, and series prefixes.

After selection, fill the v2 brief and route to `editorial-workflow.md`. Do not draft before outline approval.
